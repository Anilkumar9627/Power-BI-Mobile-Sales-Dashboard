# 📱 Mobile & Smartphone Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive **Power BI** dashboard designed to analyze smartphone sales performance, regional demand across major Indian cities, customer ratings, payment preferences, and time-based trends.

## 📊 Key KPIs & Highlights
* **Total Sales:** 769M
* **Total Quantity:** 19K units
* **Total Transactions:** 4K
* **Average Order / Transaction Value:** 40K

## 🔍 Dashboard Features & Visuals
* **Interactive Slicers & Month Navigation:** Filter data dynamically by **Mobile Model**, **Payment Method**, **Brand**, **Day Name**, or quick month buttons (**January – December**).
* **Geographic Sales Distribution:** Map visualization highlighting sales presence across cities including Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune/Indore/Bhopal regions, and more.
* **Monthly Units Sold Trend:** Line chart tracking **Sum of Units Sold** across every month of the year.
* **Top Performing Models & Brands:** 
  * Horizontal bar chart for **Total Sales by Mobile Model** (e.g., iPhone SE, OnePlus Nord, Galaxy Note 20, Vivo Y51, Galaxy S21).
  * Summary table comparing **Total Sales** and **Total Quantity** across brands (Apple, OnePlus, Samsung, Vivo, Xiaomi).
* **Payment Method Breakdown:** Pie chart showing transaction share across **UPI**, **Debit Card**, **Credit Card**, and **Cash**.
* **Customer Ratings Funnel:** Funnel chart breaking down ratings from **1 to 5** stars.
* **Day-of-Week Performance:** Area/line chart analyzing **Total Sales by Day Name** (Saturday through Wednesday).
